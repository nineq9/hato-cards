# HATO hourly news policy

HATO is a finite daily brief, not an infinite news feed.

## Cadence
- Collect candidates once per hour.
- Build a new morning edition on the first run at or after 06:00 Europe/Kyiv.
- Keep that edition stable during the day.
- During the day, update an existing story when later coverage is the same event.
- Add a new story during the day only when it reaches A-level importance.

## Volume
- Collect up to 150 candidate items per hourly run.
- Merge duplicate coverage into one event cluster.
- Morning edition: include A/B items, optionally useful C items when the edition is thin.
- Do not pad the edition to a fixed count.
- Hard maximum: 50 visible stories.
- Maximum new breaking stories added after the morning edition: 5 per hour.

## Importance
- A: major war changes, large attacks, ceasefire/diplomacy, laws/policy, major leader decisions, sanctions/alliances, critical infrastructure/energy, major military capability changes.
- B: economy, energy, budgets, reconstruction, refugees, health, education, defense industry, regional infrastructure.
- C: useful context, technology, OSINT, humanitarian/recovery developments.
- D: minor follow-ups and low-value repetition; keep as candidate context but do not publish directly.

## Editorial behavior
- Prefer updating an existing story over creating another story about the same event.
- Source diversity increases confidence/importance.
- Low-value entertainment/sports/celebrity material is filtered out.
- Known propaganda-heavy sources are not used as standalone candidate sources.
- Collection is broad; publication is selective.

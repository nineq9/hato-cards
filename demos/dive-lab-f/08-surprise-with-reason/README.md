# 08 — SURPRISE, WITH A REASON

## Hypothesis
意外な関連を理由付きで提示すると、serendipityを保ちつつ関連の誤解を減らせる可能性がある。

## Research basis
Makri & Blandford (2012) serendipity; curiosity research. Typed explanation is product inference.

## Interaction
意外な接点＋why surfacedを見て開く/閉じる→関係タイプを検証。

## Expected benefit
予想外の発見とsource transparencyの両立。

## Risk
ランダム報酬化、historical similarityを証拠扱い。

## Guardrail
why surfaced + relation type + sourceを必須化。スキップは思想推定に使わない。

## Product inference
Yes. Product benefit is an **inference** unless the cited research directly tested the same product interaction.

## Fixture / provenance
Fictional outage case only. Source types and typed relationships remain visible. AI confidence is never truth probability; historical similarity is never evidence.
